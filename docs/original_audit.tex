\section{The series}
\texttt{UIBNDSI.xlsx} holds one summary table with no formulas: \texttt{FREQUENCY}, \texttt{SUM\_AREA\_G} and a file name carrying year and day, such as \texttt{2000057.dbf}. It has 909 records from 26 February 2000 to 19 December 2019. So all processing happened before the sheet, and the ArcPy steps are not in this folder.

Most steps are 8 days. Shorter steps at year ends follow the MODIS calendar, and two 16-day steps show missing composites.

\section{The area}
The project's documents disagree about what ``Upper Indus'' means:

\begin{tabular}{lr}
\toprule
Source & Area (km$^2$) \\
\midrule
Proposal & 478,310 \\
Report & about 175,000 \\
HydroBASINS, above Tarbela (this audit) & about 164,000 \\
Largest snow area in the data & 340,549 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

Snow area in the sheet exceeds the whole basin above Tarbela, so the data cannot be that basin. Against new MOD10A2 series, the data match the Indus above Sukkur ($r=0.991$, median ratio 0.99), and not the basin above Tarbela ($r=0.927$, ratio 1.78). The sheet is therefore about a larger area than the report describes. The basin above Sukkur totals about 334,700~km$^2$, slightly below the sheet's largest snow area, so the true boundary is probably larger still, and may be the proposal's 478,310~km$^2$. That boundary file is not in this folder.

\section{Trend}
Annual means from the original data show no significant trend ($\tau=0.20$, $p=0.24$), and a straight line through the 8-day values explains 0.04\% of their variance, since the seasonal cycle dominates. The new series agree on the absence of a significant trend.

\section{Report}
The report contains statements about 2003 and 2013, and about 2005 and 2015, which do not arise from a 2000--2019 analysis, and it uses images taken from other websites. Its text must not be reused, as already recorded. Nothing from it enters the manuscript.
